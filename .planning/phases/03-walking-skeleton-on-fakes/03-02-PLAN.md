<objective>
Add the only case-file route the renderer may use, `app://bundle/cases/<id>/interviewee.pdf`, plus the deck-text source the session needs for `loadCase`.

Purpose: BAR-02. `app://` never serves an interviewer package. The route lives on the `bundle` host on purpose: the renderer's origin is `app://bundle`, and the scheme has no `corsEnabled` (a Phase 1 decision). Phase 4's react-pdf can then fetch the deck same-origin without weakening CORS. This is the concrete URL shape for the CONTEXT decision "allowlisted roots: bundle, plus cases/<validCaseId>/interviewee.pdf" (Claude's discretion on the exact URL).
