readConfig rules:
- adapters: env.ADAPTERS ?? 'fake'. Any other value, or LLM/STT/TTS set to anything but 'fake', throws `Error('adapter not available in this build: <NAME>=<value>')`. Real adapters land in Phases 5-7.
- casesRoot: env.CASES_ROOT || DEFAULT_LIBRARY_ROOT
- sessionsDir: env.SESSIONS_DIR || join(userData, 'sessions')
- e2e: env.E2E === '1'
- timeScale: e2e && Number(env.E2E_TIME_SCALE) > 0 ? Number(env.E2E_TIME_SCALE) : 1. It is never honoured outside E2E.
