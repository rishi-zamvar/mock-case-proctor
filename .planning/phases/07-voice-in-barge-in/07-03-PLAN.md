<objective>
The renderer half of push-to-talk capture. It has three parts: a pure accumulator (chunking at the quietest frame, a 5-minute cap, and tail flush), a copy-only AudioWorklet, and a `createMicCapture` glue module. The glue owns one 16 kHz AudioContext, the mic stream (echoCancellation, noiseSuppression and autoGainControl all on), mute, and the E2E-only `window.__mic` WAV injection hook. It also moves `brevityBand` to `src/shared` so the renderer can colour the answer timer.

Purpose: VIN-01 capture, and the renderer side of chunked transcription (VOUT-04 latency).
Output: `src/renderer/src/mic/*`, the worklet in `public/`, and `src/shared/brevity.ts`.
